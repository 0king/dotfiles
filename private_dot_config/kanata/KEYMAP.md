# Kanata Keymap

### Base Layer
* **`Caps` / `Esc`**: Tap `Esc` | Hold **Mouse**
* **`Space`**: Tap `Space` | Hold **Alt**
* **`J + K`** (chord): `Esc`
* **HRM (Home Row Mods)**:
  * Left: `A` (Super) · `S` (Alt) · `D` (Ctrl) · `F` (Shift)
  * Right: `J` (Shift) · `K` (Ctrl) · `L` (Alt) · `;` (Super)
* **Vim Repeat**: Double-tap (tap into hold) on `J`, `K`, `L` disables HRM and repeats character
* *Physical Mods held*: Drops HRMs (`no-hrm`)

---

### Alt Layer *(Hold `Space`)*
* **Nav**: `E S D F` = `↑ ← ↓ →` | `H / G` = `Home / End` | `R / V` = `PgUp / PgDn`
* **Edit**: `A` = `Enter` | `C` = `Space` | `X` = `Backspace` | `Z` = `Del` | `T` = `Tab`
* **Chars**: `Q` = `` ` `` | `W` = `~` | `1..=` = Symbols (`!` `@` `#` …)
* **Function**: `F1 / F2` = Brightness `-/+` | `F3` = `PrintScreen` | `F11 / F12` = Volume `-/+`

---

### Mouse Layer *(Hold `Caps` or `Esc`)*
* **Cursor**: `I J K L` = `↑ ← ↓ →` | `C` (Fast) · `V` (Slow)
* **Scroll**: `E S D F` = `↑ ← ↓ →` | `N / M` = `↑ / ↓` | `O / P` = Top / Bottom | `U` (Fast) · `Y` (Slow)
* **Clicks**: `Space` = Left Click | `R` = Right Click

---

### Toggles
* **`Meta + Esc`**: **Media Layer** (Arrows on `ESDF`/`IJKL`, `1/2` = Brightness, `- /=` = Volume)
* **`Meta + Ctrl + Esc`**: **Bypass Layer** (Native physical keyboard, bypasses Kanata)
